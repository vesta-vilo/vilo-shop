I lead Vilo’s vision, product, brand, and industrial design direction.

I am a founder who has spent the last decade building physical products across robotics, premium home, and AI wearables. My work has always lived somewhere between technology and emotion: how a thing works, how it feels in the hand, how it sits in a room, and why people keep it close.

Before Vilo, I was a core member of Vincross, a consumer robotics company that built HEXA, a six-legged robot recognized by global media and later acquired by Lenovo. I then built Vesta, a premium bedding and lifestyle brand serving more than 350,000 customers, most of them women, across North America.

Vilo brings those worlds together: robotics-grade hardware and software, the material and form discipline of industrial design, and the taste, trust, and daily intimacy of a premium consumer brand. My role is to keep the product honest: technically ambitious, emotionally clear, and built for the long arc of daily life.
