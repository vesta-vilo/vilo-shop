I lead Vilo's vision, product, brand, and design.

For a decade I've built physical things across robotics, premium home, and AI wearables, always somewhere between technology and emotion. How a thing works. How it feels in the hand. Why people keep it close.

Before Vilo, I was a core member of Vincross, the robotics company behind HEXA, a six-legged robot covered around the world and later acquired by Lenovo. Then I built Vesta, a premium bedding brand now in more than 350,000 homes across North America, most of them women.

Vilo is where those worlds meet: robotics-grade hardware, the discipline of industrial design, and the taste of a brand you actually want to wear.

My job is easy to say and hard to do. Keep the product honest, and worth keeping for years.
