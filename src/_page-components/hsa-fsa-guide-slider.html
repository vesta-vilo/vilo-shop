Step 1 — Choose HSA/FSA at Checkout
Select “Pay with HSA/FSA” during checkout.
It only takes a minute and you'll be guided through the rest.
Step 2 — Complete a Quick Health Survey
Answer a few simple questions about your health goals.
A licensed provider will review your responses to determine eligibility.
Step 3 — Check Out as Usual
Pay using your credit or debit card.
If approved, TrueMed will provide everything you need to request reimbursement from your HSA/FSA provider.
