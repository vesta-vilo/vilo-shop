The Essentials

What is Vilo?

Vilo is an agent-first ring. It reads your sleep, heart, energy, and cycle quietly in the background, and returns it as calm, personal guidance — not another wall of charts. Worn like jewelry, run by Signal OS.

Is Vilo a medical device?

No. Vilo is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

What it offers is context, not a verdict. That said, we take the science seriously — our sensing draws on established research methods and recognized reference standards, so the engine is rigorous even as the experience stays calm and human.

Do I need a subscription?

The ring and its core features work out of the box. Deeper, personalized guidance — the part where the agent learns your baseline and speaks at the right moment — runs on ongoing AI compute, and that's what Membership covers. You own the ring; Membership keeps the intelligence growing.

Everyday Sensing

What does Vilo actually read?

Five streams, one calm read: your sleep, your heart (rate and HRV), your skin temperature, your movement, and your cycle. Signal OS draws them together so you see the pattern, not the noise.

How does sleep tracking work?

While you sleep, Vilo follows your heart rate, HRV, movement, and temperature to map your nights — deep sleep, when your body does its repair; REM, when your mind sorts the day; and the light sleep in between. In the morning, it surfaces what mattered and points to the next step. It can also wake you with a gentle haptic alarm — no sound, no startle.

What does it tell me about my heart?

Vilo reads your heart rate and heart-rate variability through the day and night. HRV tends to fall when you're stressed, tired, or running down — so when yours shifts, Vilo gives you the context to ease off or push on. Trends, read over time, not a single alarming number.

Does it track my cycle?

Yes — quietly. After ovulation your body temperature rises slightly, and your resting heart rate tends to climb through the second half of your cycle. That's physiology, not guesswork. Vilo follows those shifts across your month and carries the context forward, so a restless night or a low afternoon makes more sense.

Does it track workouts and daily activity?

Yes. Vilo senses movement day and night and folds your activity into the bigger picture of energy and recovery — so effort and rest are read together, the way your body actually experiences them.

How accurate is it?

Vilo's sensing is built on methods drawn from published research and measured against recognized reference standards. We hold the engine to clinical-grade rigor and keep the read human.

Privacy & Your Data

Who can see my health data?

You. Vilo is built so your most personal signals — sleep, heart, cycle — stay yours. We don't sell your data, and we don't trade it to advertisers. Full stop.

Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and tied to your account, not your name on a list somewhere.

Can I delete my data?

Anytime. You can export or permanently delete your data from the app whenever you choose. It's your record to keep — or to erase.

Do you share my cycle data?

No. Cycle and reproductive data is among the most sensitive a person carries, and we treat it that way — never sold, never shared with advertisers, fully yours to delete.
