Your cycle, finally in context.

Not every change needs a warning. After ovulation your body temperature rises slightly, and your resting heart rate tends to climb through the second half of your cycle. That's physiology, not guesswork. Vilo follows those shifts from your ear across the month and carries the context forward, so a restless night or a low afternoon makes more sense.

Never take it off to charge it.

Each Vilo Earring comes with two swappable batteries, and a single battery lasts over seven days. Once a week, swap in your charged backup and drop the empty one in the charger for next time. No overnight charging. No gap in your data. No missed nights of sleep.
